The fix copies that original duplicate-removal/missing-flag block into `SimCore.UpdateMovementFrame`, after the original HTF bookkeeping and outside the bullet-time guard. It retains descending duplicate removal, alpha-before-bravo order, `RandomizeStart` calls, source constants and RNG consumption. The fixture is not padded with a blue flag to conceal the omission.

## Infrastructure boundary

Network, console/logging and demo notifications are not gameplay implementations. Their replacements emit deterministic ordered events where relevant, never perform host I/O during a tick and never consume RNG. Scripts and unsupported modes remain unavailable. Original waypoint AI is admitted in ABI 4 under the explicit boundaries above. A successful compilation alone does not establish this ledger's gate review or the T1/T2/T3 results.
