## Infrastructure boundary

Network, console/logging and demo notifications are not gameplay implementations. Their replacements must emit deterministic ordered events where relevant, never perform host I/O during a tick, never consume RNG, and never silently stand in for a required gameplay operation. Scripts, AI and unsupported modes are explicitly unavailable in the Phase 1 ABI. A successful compilation alone does not establish this ledger's gate review or the T1/T2/T3 results.
