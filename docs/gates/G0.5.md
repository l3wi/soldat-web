## Render-spike handoff and clarified ordering

Output schema and consumer rules are in `packages/content/README.md`. `dist/content` contains all source-ordered map vertices/UVs/RGBA, raw normals, props, scenery styles, colliders, spawnpoints, weather/background and edge/terrain references, plus raw simulation bytes and exported gostek/animation data.

PNG derivatives intentionally retain **straight alpha**; consumers premultiply exactly once at GPU upload. Terrain is never green-keyed, keyed sprites/edges/scenery follow source rules, animated GIFs are not keyed, and NPOT dimensions are retained. This clarifies the earlier “green key + premultiply” wording, not a claim that decoded PNG bytes are already premultiplied. Full native screenshot equivalence and animation evaluation are G0.4/Phase 1 work, not established by parsing success.
