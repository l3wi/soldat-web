## Save-state performance

The codec now encodes/validates once into reusable grow-only storage, hashes contiguous canonical bytes and reuses that snapshot/digest only until the next mutating ABI entry. Every mutation invalidates before potential partial changes; load invalidates internally. No gameplay field is dropped. Existing canonical FNV semantics retain a zero transport-length word and exclude previous render-pose coordinates.

Measured on Node `v24.14.0`, Darwin arm64, two players, tick 60, 100 warmups and 500 measured samples, with a real set_input invalidation **before every sample** and outside timing:

- Final-artifact best observed save+hash p50: **1.313833 ms**; p95 **1.398375 ms**, **173,216 bytes**. Mean **1.320860 ms**; cached hash-after-save p50 **0.000208 ms**.
- Earlier ABI-2 measurements were p50 **1.34625 ms** / p95 **1.426959 ms** at 173,152 bytes, and p50 **1.360458 ms** / p95 **1.508708 ms** after adding explicit loadout choices.
- These improve the user-reported approximately 10 ms baseline substantially, but **≤1 ms is not achieved**. No warm-cache-only benchmark is presented as a full save+hash result.

The final artifact-bound benchmark is persisted at `build-out/sim/g3/state-performance.json`. It measures a fully invalidated encode/hash for each sample, not just the cache lookup.
