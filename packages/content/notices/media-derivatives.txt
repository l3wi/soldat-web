Browser media derivatives
=========================

The source OpenSoldat base assets retain their original CC-BY-4.0 license and
attribution. See base-license.txt and base-credits.txt alongside this notice.
The original source tables retain the MIT notice in opensoldat-mit.txt. The Play
font is copied unchanged under play-OFL.txt. These transformations do not imply
endorsement by the original contributors.

Images: every composited RGBA8 frame is encoded with cwebp -lossless -exact -z 9.
The importer independently decodes every WebP with dwebp and rejects any change
to dimensions or straight-alpha RGBA bytes, including RGB under transparent
pixels. Frame timing, native scale, color-key behavior, and NPOT dimensions are
preserved. No premultiplication is baked into the derivative.

Audio: original WAV files are build-time inputs only. Microsoft ADPCM is first
decoded to PCM with its original fact sample count; PCM inputs are read without
changing the source masters. Browser derivatives are Ogg Opus, libopus 64 kbps
VBR, mono, 48 kHz. This is a lossy transformation; original sample values are not
preserved. Original and encoded duration metadata, Opus pre-skip and final Ogg
granule are recorded. Duration deviation must not exceed one 48-kHz sample.

Native loop samples are sfx/rocketz.wav (2), sfx/flamer.wav (56), and
sfx/chainsaw-r.wav (133), as selected in client/Sound.pas. Loop derivatives are
not crossfaded, padded, or trimmed to hide discontinuities. The importer records
normalized source/decoded seam jumps, first/last five-millisecond RMS, and
adjacent-sample delta RMS. These are objective signal statistics, not audible
listening or a claim that lossy encoding is perceptually indistinguishable.

The complete source repository URL, pinned revision, source path, source hash,
and ordered transformation records remain in manifest.json's objects index.
Raw simulation payloads and sim/manifest.json serialization are not changed by
media encoding or by the SPK1 transport envelopes.
